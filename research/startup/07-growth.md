# Growth: the first 1,000 creators with $0 ad budget

The year-one goal of ~2,000 payers needs ~50,000 free signups (see
[06-business.md](06-business.md)). There is no ad budget, so every channel below runs on founder
time and on the product's own output.

## Channels

| Channel | What we do | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Build in public** | A daily before/after reel on the founders' channels (raw clip → finished reel). A weekly live "my AI edits this video" session | The product makes the content. Every post is a demo |
| **Creator partners** | 20 mid-size educators (20k–300k followers) get free Pro + a 20–30% affiliate cut and show raw → edited on camera | Educators trust other educators; their audiences are full of educators |
| **Template pages** | Each caption style and graphic template gets a public page with a sample reel and a "use this style" button | Search and social hooks ("Hormozi-style captions", "lower third template") |
| **Watermark loop** | The free tier exports with a small "made with …" mark | Every free reel posted is an ad |
| **AI directories** | List the connector in Claude's directory, ChatGPT apps and MCP registries | People searching "video" inside their AI find us |
| **Launch moments** | Product Hunt at month 3; posts in creator and indie-hacker communities | Bursts of signups and backlinks |

## Creator partner program

| Item | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Who | Talking-head educators, 20k–300k followers, posting 3+ times a week |
| Offer | Free Pro for life + 20–30% of revenue from their referrals for 12 months |
| Ask | One raw → edited video showing the product, plus honest feedback calls during the beta |
| How many | 20 in the first 3 months |
| Cost | Free plans + ~$500 total in gifts/boosts (see the budget) |

## Funnel targets

| Step | Target | Measured how |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor → signup | 10% | Landing page analytics |
| Signup → first export within 15 min | 50% | Product event: first export timestamp |
| Free → paid | 4% | Billing data |
| Paying users still active after month 1 | 60% | Billing + usage |

## Weekly rhythm (both founders)

| Day | Growth task |
|---|---|
| Daily | One before/after reel posted; reply to every comment and DM |
| Monday | Pick 5 new creator partners to contact |
| Wednesday | Publish one new template page |
| Friday | Live "AI edits this video" session; review the funnel numbers |

## The one demo that has to sell it

A 30-second video:
1. A raw, messy talking-head clip.
2. One sentence typed into Claude.
3. A finished reel with designed captions, zooms and graphics.
4. Someone clicks a caption and changes it, to show it is still editable.

Make this video before anything else; it is the landing page, the pitch and the first post.
