# Week-0 educator interview script

**Goal:** find out whether talking-head educators have a painful, expensive editing problem
we can solve, **before** writing product code.

**Gate** (from [05-roadmap.md](05-roadmap.md)): 10+ of 15–20 educators show a specific pain
**and** at least one of:
- they already spend money or hours on it, or
- they make a commitment at the end of the call.

Saying "cool idea" doesn't count.

## Rules for the interviewer

These follow *The Mom Test* (Rob Fitzpatrick): people lie about the future but not about the
past.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask about the **last time** they did something | Ask "would you use…?" or "would you pay…?" |
| Ask what it cost them: hours, money, people | Pitch the product before the last 5 minutes |
| Dig into emotion: "why was that annoying?" | Accept compliments as data |
| Let silence sit; they'll fill it | Ask leading questions ("Isn't captioning painful?") |
| Ask for proof: "can you show me?" | Talk more than ~20% of the call |

**Roles:**
- One founder asks, the other takes notes in the template at the bottom.
- Record only with permission.
- Each call is **30 minutes**.

## Who to interview

| Criterion | Target |
|---|---|
| Format | Films themselves talking to camera (coaching, finance, tech, fitness, language, medicine…) |
| Size | 5k–300k followers on at least one platform |
| Frequency | Posts 3+ short videos a week, or 1+ long video a week |
| Mix | ~⅓ edit themselves, ~⅓ pay an editor, ~⅓ use CapCut/Submagic/Descript |
| Niches | At least 3 different niches (answers question 1 in [09-open-questions.md](09-open-questions.md)) |

Write the list of 20 names before sending any message (question 10).

## Recruiting messages

**DM (Instagram / X / LinkedIn):**

> Hi {name} — I'm {founder}, a video editor building tools for educators who film themselves.
> I loved your video on {specific topic}. Could I ask you 20 minutes of questions about how you
> edit your videos? Not selling anything. Happy to edit one of your clips for free as a thank-you.

**Follow-up after 3 days (once only):**

> Hi {name}, quick bump on this. 20 minutes, your schedule, and a free edit of any clip you
> choose. Totally fine if not.

**Thank-you offer:** a free professional edit of one of their clips by the motion-designer
cofounder. It is good for the relationship and becomes a before/after post (with permission).

## The script (30 minutes)

### 1. Opening (2 min)

> Thanks for doing this. I'm trying to understand how educators like you make videos today:
> what works, what's annoying. There are no right answers, and nothing to buy. Mind if I record
> it for my notes?

### 2. Context (4 min)

1. What do you teach, and who watches you?
2. Which platforms matter most to you right now, and why?
3. How many videos did you post last week? Short, long, or both?
4. Does your content make you money? How? (Sponsors, courses, coaching, clients.)

### 3. The last video, step by step (10 min)

The most important section. Get the real story of **one specific recent video**.

5. Think of the last video you posted. Walk me through it from recording to posting.
6. How long did each part take: recording, cutting, captions, graphics, exporting, posting?
7. Who did the editing? (You, an editor, an assistant, a tool?)
8. Which tools did you use, in what order?
9. Did you turn one recording into several videos? How?
10. What part did you put off or rush? Why?
11. Can you show me that video, and maybe the raw footage?

### 4. Pain and spend (6 min)

12. What's the most annoying part of editing for you? Tell me about the last time it happened.
13. What have you tried to fix it? (Tools, editors, templates, courses.)
14. Why did you stop using {tool they tried}?
15. What do you pay today, in money per month, for editing tools and people?
16. If you pay an editor: how did you find them, what do you pay per video, what goes wrong?
17. Have you ever paid for something that promised to fix this? What happened?

### 5. Quality: what "cheap" and "AI slop" mean to them (4 min)

18. Show me a video (yours or someone else's) whose editing you admire. What makes it good?
19. Show me one that looks cheap or "AI-made" to you. What gives it away?
20. Do you have brand colors, fonts or a logo you use every time?
21. Have you ever deleted or redone an edit because it looked off? What was wrong?

### 6. AI habits (2 min)

22. Do you use ChatGPT, Claude or another AI for your content today? For what?
23. Which plan do you pay for, if any? (Answers question 30: does MCP reach free users?)
24. Have you tried any AI editing tool? What happened?

### 7. Pitch and commitment test (2 min)

Only now describe the product, in one or two sentences:

> We're building an editor where you drop in your raw footage, tell your AI "make this a
> reel in my style", and get designed captions, zooms and graphics. Every piece stays
> editable.

Then ask for **one** commitment, from strongest to weakest:

| Commitment | Counts as |
|---|---|
| Pre-pay for the founding-member plan (~$149/year) | Strong |
| Send 2–3 raw videos for us to test with | Strong |
| Join the private beta in week 7 and give feedback | Medium |
| Introduce us to 2 other educators | Medium |
| "Keep me posted" | **Zero** (it's a polite no) |

Close with:

> Who else should I talk to? Can I follow up when we have something to show you?

## Scoring and synthesis (after every 5 interviews)

| Score | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain** | Mild annoyance, no workaround | Clear pain, tried a fix | Hates it, spends a lot to fix it |
| **Spend** | $0 and < 1 h/week | $20–50/mo or 3–5 h/week | Pays an editor or $50+/mo, or 8+ h/week |

| Commitment | Points |
|---|---|
| None | 0 |
| Beta or intro | 1 |
| Footage | 2 |
| Pre-pay | 3 |

After each batch of 5:
1. Tally top pains: count how many named each pain unprompted.
2. List the "cheap / AI-made" signals. These become the first quality-lint rules in
   [02-positioning.md](02-positioning.md).
3. Note the admired references. They brief the first caption styles and templates.
4. Update the answers in [09-open-questions.md](09-open-questions.md) (questions 1–10, 23, 30,
   51).

**Decision after 15–20 interviews:**

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| 10+ with pain ≥ 4 and (spend ≥ 3 or commitment ≥ 1) | **Go:** start the week-1 spikes |
| 5–9 meet that bar | Narrow to the niche or segment that scored highest; interview 10 more there |
| Under 5 | Stop. Re-think the buyer (freelance editors? agencies?) before building |
